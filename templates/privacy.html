Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ effective_date }}. This page explains what Crop Sentry collects, why, how long it is kept, and your choices.

Plain-language summary

  • We collect the location you ask us to evaluate (ZIP, town, or coordinates), the crop and field options you choose, basic technical metadata about your request, and a record that you accepted our Terms.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not run advertising trackers.
  • Saved fields and theme settings live in your browser’s local storage on your device, not on our servers.
  • We share data only with the third-party providers needed to run the service (forecast, soil, geocoding, hosting, payments).

1. What we collect

a. Information you provide

  • Location input — ZIP code, town name, or latitude/longitude you enter or click on the map. Locations resolve to coordinates we send to weather, soil, and geocoding providers.
  • Field options — crop, tillage, residue cover, manure, prior grass, residual herbicide, and similar inputs.
  • Account information (paid tiers, when offered) — name, email, billing details handled by our payment processor.
  • Support correspondence — anything you send us by email or contact form.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Request metadata — IP address, user-agent string, timestamps, and request paths, used for security, abuse prevention, debugging, and aggregate analytics.
  • Acknowledgment record — when you accept the Terms, we log the timestamp, the version of the Terms accepted, and a truncated/hashed form of your IP for evidentiary purposes (so we can show that consent occurred).
  • Cookies / local storage — see section 5.

c. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect government IDs, financial-account numbers (handled by our processor), or biometric data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers or behavioral-ad pixels.

2. How we use information

  • to operate the service (resolve a location, fetch forecast and soil data, score risk);
  • to improve the model — only on de-identified or aggregated query metadata (e.g., counts of evaluations by region, error rates by data source);
  • to keep the service secure and prevent abuse, including rate-limiting and fraud detection;
  • to communicate with you about service changes, paid features, or support requests you initiate;
  • to comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use the contents of your queries or saved fields to train third-party AI models or to profile you for advertising.

3. Who we share with

We share information only with the following categories of recipients, and only as needed to operate the service:

  • Weather and agronomic data providers — including Open-Meteo, NWS api.weather.gov, NASA POWER, USDA SSURGO/NRCS, the U.S. Drought Monitor, Iowa State University ICM, and similar public sources. Coordinates and the request itself are sent to these providers; they receive only what is necessary to return data for that point.
  • Geocoding — to convert ZIPs and place names into coordinates and back.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — for serving the website and API.
  • Payment processor (paid tiers) — for subscription and billing. We do not store full card numbers; the processor handles those directly.
  • Legal compliance — when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety.
  • Successors — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case the acquirer must honor commitments at least as protective as this policy.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

4. How long we keep information

  • Server logs — typically up to 90 days, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Acknowledgment records — retained for the duration of the limitations period in any jurisdiction in which a claim could plausibly be brought (generally up to seven years), so that we can demonstrate you accepted the Terms.
  • Account and billing records (paid tiers) — retained for the life of the account plus the period required by tax and accounting law.
  • Saved fields and theme — stored locally in your browser; you can clear them at any time from the dashboard or your browser settings.

5. Cookies and local storage

Crop Sentry uses a small number of strictly-necessary client-side stores:

  • cropsentry.theme — remembers light/dark preference.
  • cropsentry.savedFields — your saved field shortcuts on the dashboard.
  • cropsentry.tosAccepted — records that you accepted the Terms so we don’t prompt again.

These are stored in your browser, not on our servers, and they do not contain personal identifiers beyond what you typed. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction or deletion;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • request a portable copy;
  • withdraw consent at any time (where we relied on consent);
  • lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

California residents have specific rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and limit use of sensitive information. Residents of the EEA, U.K., and Switzerland have rights under the GDPR. To exercise any right, contact us at the address in section 10.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information, including TLS in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed without authorization.

8. Children

The service is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that threshold applies). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Crop Sentry is operated from the United States. If you access the service from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S. and other countries where our providers operate. We rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers where required by law.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or any of the rights described above, please email cjames2434@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted on the dashboard or by email if you have an account.

This Privacy Policy is provided as a starting point and does not constitute legal advice. Have it reviewed by counsel familiar with your jurisdictions and the privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, etc.) that apply to your business before launch.
