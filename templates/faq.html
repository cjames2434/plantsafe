Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Crop Sentry.

What is Crop Sentry?

Crop Sentry is a free decision-support tool that scores planting risk for corn and soybeans. It pulls real-time weather forecasts, soil survey data, and pest biofix reports to evaluate 14 separate risk factors — from imbibitional chilling to seedcorn maggot pressure — and gives you a single recommendation: Optimal, Wait & Watch, or Do Not Plant.

Is Crop Sentry free?

Yes. Crop Sentry is completely free. All data comes from public government sources (USDA, NOAA/NWS, NASA POWER, ISU ICM) and the open-source Open-Meteo API. No credit card or account is required.

Where does the data come from?

Every assessment pulls from multiple authoritative sources:

  • USDA SSURGO — Soil drainage class, hydrologic group, texture, and organic matter
  • NOAA / National Weather Service — Grid-point forecasts and active weather alerts (frost, flood, severe)
  • NASA POWER (MERRA-2) — Reanalysis data for 3-source cross-checking
  • Open-Meteo — Hourly forecast, 30-day archive, and multi-model ensemble uncertainty
  • ISU ICM — Black cutworm pheromone-trap network for GDD biofix modeling
  • USDM — US Drought Monitor intensity data

Sources are surfaced on every results page so you can see exactly what informed the recommendation.

How accurate is it?

Crop Sentry cross-checks every forecast against at least two independent sources (NWS and NASA POWER) to catch model drift. The survival percentage includes a confidence interval derived from multi-model ensemble dispersion (GFS, ICON, ECMWF, GEM).

That said, Crop Sentry is decision support, not professional advice. Field conditions can vary from any model — we always recommend verifying with a local agronomist or extension agent before making final planting decisions.

What risk factors are evaluated?

Crop Sentry scores 14 risk factors grouped into 6 independent hazard categories:

  • Weather stress — Imbibitional chilling, frost, flooding/waterlogging, soil crusting
  • Diseases — Pythium (damping-off), Phytophthora (soybean root rot)
  • Insects — Seedcorn maggot, wireworm, slugs, black cutworm, bean leaf beetle
  • Soil history — Antecedent saturation (prior 30-day moisture loading)
  • Topography — Elevation concavity (water ponding risk)
  • Chemical — Herbicide carryover from the previous season

See the Methodology page for the full math and thresholds.

What areas does Crop Sentry cover?

Crop Sentry works for any location in the continental United States. Enter a ZIP code, town name, or click directly on the interactive map. Data sources have best coverage in the Midwest Corn Belt, but forecasts and soil data are available nationwide.

Does selecting a seed brand change the results?

Yes. When you pick a specific cultivar from the seed catalog, Crop Sentry adjusts the soil-temperature floor, frost threshold, and pathogen evaluators based on that hybrid's published trait class — emergence score, cold tolerance, and maturity group. This can shift the survival percentage by several points.

How do I save and compare fields?

After running an evaluation, tap Save field on the results page. Saved fields appear on your Dashboard for quick re-evaluation. You can also drop multiple pins on the Map page to compare risk scores side by side. All saved data stays in your browser — we don't store it on our servers.

Do you sell my data?

No. Crop Sentry does not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We collect minimal request metadata (location input, field options) retained for 90 days to improve the service. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I use the API directly?

Yes. Crop Sentry exposes a JSON API at /api/evaluate that accepts the same parameters as the web interface. Visit /docs for the auto-generated OpenAPI documentation. The API is rate-limited to 30 requests per minute per IP.
